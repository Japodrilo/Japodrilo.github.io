I am a Mathematician and Computer Scientist mainly interested in Graph Theory.

Since January 2024 I am a tenured "Profesor Titular A" (roughly equivalent to
Associate Professor) at the Mathematics Department of the Faculty of Science at
the National Autonomous University of México (UNAM). Before that I was a tenure
track "Profesor Asociado C" since December 2019. I was a CINVESTAV Researcher
at the [Department of Computer Science](https://wwwcs.cs.cinvestav.mx/) at
CINVESTAV between 2018 and 2019. During 2016 I was a Cátedra CONACYT Researcher,
commissioned to the [Unidad Académica de
Matemáticas](https://matematicas.reduaz.mx/web/) at UAZ. I also held postdoc
positions at UNAM working first with [Gabriela
Araujo](https://www.matem-juriquilla.unam.mx/Personales/GabrielaAraujo) and
later with Rita Zuazua. During 2012 I was a postdoctoral fellow at the [School
of Computing Science](https://www.sfu.ca/fas/computing.html) at Simon Fraser
University under the supervision of [Pavol Hell](https://www.cs.sfu.ca/~pavol/).

I obtained my Ph.D. and my M.Sc. at the [Institute of
Mathematics](https://www.matem.unam.mx/) at UNAM under the supervision of
Hortensia Galeana Sánchez. I hold B.Sc. in Mathematics and Computer Science,
both from the Faulty of Science at UNAM.

I currently teach at both the Mathematics and Computer Science undergraduate
programs in the Faculty of Science at UNAM. I also participate teaching and
supervising students at the graduate programs in
[Mathematics](https://matematicas.posgrado.unam.mx/) and [Computer Science and
Engineering](https://www.pcic.unam.mx/). I usually teach courses in Graph
Theory and Theoretical Computer Science, but I also enjoy teaching Algebra
courses.

Currently, my main lines of research lie within structural graph theory, with an
emphasis on [hereditary classes of
graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hereditary_property) (characterizations in
terms of forbidden induced subgraphs, recognition algorithms, hardness results,
etc.). Since my Ph.D. I have also worked on digraphs, principally (but not
restricted to) kernels in digraphs and similar concepts.
